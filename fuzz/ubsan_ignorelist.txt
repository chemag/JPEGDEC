# Signed overflow that fuzzing builds deliberately ignore.
#
# JPEGIDCT() multiplies dequantized coefficients by fixed-point constants in
# int. Corrupt coefficients or quantizer tables overflow those multiplies;
# the result is garbage pixels for garbage input, and the IDCT is kept free of
# range checks for speed. Every other signed overflow stays fatal.
[signed-integer-overflow]
fun:JPEGIDCT
